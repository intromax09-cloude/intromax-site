    Questions we get asked before the start

    How much does it cost and what does the price depend on?

    Until 1 November 2026 launch prices apply: a basic bot is $70–140, an AI agent $180–280, a website $70–250 depending on scope, an automation scenario $70–200. After that date the regular prices return: $100–200, $250–400, $100–350 and $100–300.

    A basic bot is $100–200, an AI agent $250–400, a website $100–350 depending on scope, an automation scenario $100–300.

    Within the range, the figure depends on the number of script branches, the integrations needed and whether texts and photos are ready. We give you the exact amount within a day of your request, before work starts.

    How long does the launch take?

    A basic bot takes 2–3 days. A one-page website up to a week. An AI agent or extended website 1–2 weeks. An automation scenario from one day if access is already in place. All we need from you is 10 minutes for a brief and answers to a few questions.

    Do you need my Instagram password?

    No. The connection is official, through the Instagram button inside the service: you log in with your own account and press “allow”. We never see the password, the account is not moved anywhere, and you can revoke access at any moment with one button.

    What if Instagram blocks my account because of the bot?

    Accounts get blocked for mass messaging strangers. The bot only replies to people who wrote to you first and works through the official Meta connection, which is an allowed scenario. We do not do or promise broadcasts to an old base in Instagram: that feature lives in Telegram, where it is legal.

    What happens if the bot does not understand a customer?

    It does not invent an answer. If the question is outside the script, it says “I will pass this to a human” and leaves the message for you. Every bot also has a “call a human” button: after it the bot goes quiet in that dialogue so it does not get in the way of your live reply.

    Can I see how it works first?

    Yes, and not on someone else’s example but on yours. After your request we build a demo for your task: a bot with your services and prices, a page mockup or a diagram of the automation chain. You get the demo as a link: open it, click around, ask the bot an awkward question and see what it answers.

    It is free and comes with no obligation. If after the demo you decide you do not need it, that is fine: at least you have seen what it looks like from the inside.

    What is n8n automation in plain words?

    It is when the services you already use start passing data to each other without you. For example: a customer leaves a request on the website → it automatically appears in a spreadsheet or CRM → you get a Telegram notification → a day before the visit the customer gets a reminder. Nobody copies anything by hand and nothing gets lost.

    Who pays for the platform and is there a mandatory subscription?

    The bot lives on an official licensed messaging platform; the basic free plan is enough for most small businesses. If your volume outgrows the free limits, we warn you in advance and show the real price of the plan: you pay the service directly, not us. The website is hosted for free, the domain costs $10–15 a year paid to the registrar.

    What does monthly support include and is it mandatory?

    It is not mandatory: the bot and website work without it. It includes edits to scripts and texts, making sure nothing breaks after platform updates, minor improvements without a separate invoice and a reply from us within a day. It does not include a new bot for another account, website development or advertising: those are separate jobs, and we say so right away.

    Do you have cases and reviews?

    Intromax is only starting to work with clients, so there are few reviews yet, and we will not make them up. Instead there is what you can check right now: two delivered projects with live links in the Cases section, a Direct bot and a transparent price list.

    How does payment work?

    In stages: part at the start, the rest after you have seen a working result. The price is fixed before we begin and does not grow along the way. We agree on a convenient payment method in the chat: just tell us what suits you.

    Who this is for, and who it is not

    If we see there is nothing to automate, we say so.

    Worth doing if

    • You answer the same few questions every day
    • Half of the messages arrive outside working hours
    • Customers have nowhere to see prices, everything lives in chats
    • Leads live in chats and notes, and some of them get lost
    • You move the same data by hand from place to place every day

    Not worth it if

    • You get two or three enquiries a week and can handle them yourself
    • Every order is unique and needs a live conversation
    • Customers come from calls and referrals, and your online presence is empty
    • You expect “a bot that sells instead of me” or “a website that brings customers on its own”; that does not happen
